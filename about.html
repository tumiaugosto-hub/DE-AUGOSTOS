About Us

Our story, mission, and commitment to excellence

Who We Are

DE AUGOSTOS INVESTMENTS is a premier automotive service provider specializing in German-manufactured machines. With a passion for engineering excellence and precision, we have established ourselves as a trusted partner for car owners, individual mechanics, and businesses across Botswana.

Our team comprises highly skilled technicians with extensive experience in BMW, Mercedes-Benz, Audi, and Volkswagen vehicles. We combine factory-grade diagnostic equipment with traditional mechanical expertise to deliver unmatched service quality.

Founded on the principles of integrity, quality, and customer satisfaction, we strive to exceed expectations with every vehicle that comes through our doors.

Our Vision

To become the best and most recognized automobile business for German-manufactured machines by the year 2035.

Our Mission

To provide exceptional automotive services that enhance the performance, safety, and longevity of German-engineered vehicles, while building lasting relationships with our customers through trust and reliability.
